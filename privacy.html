Privacy policy.

Live Meters application and support website.

Houghton Audio · Effective October 11, 2026

Live Meters application

Live Meters does not collect or transmit user data. It does not use analytics or crash reporting, record or save audio, or require an account.

Live Meters reads audio from your selected Core Audio input device solely to calculate and display Peak and RMS meter levels. Monitored audio is not recorded, saved, or transmitted by the app.

Support correspondence

If you choose to email live-meters-support@houghtonaudio.com, your email includes your address and any information or attachments you provide. Support correspondence is separate from the app’s operation. Please avoid sending sensitive information.

For questions about the handling or deletion of support correspondence, contact Houghton Audio at the address above.

This support website

This support website does not include analytics or advertising scripts, set cookies, provide contact forms, or load external fonts. Its email links open your email application.

When hosted on GitHub Pages, GitHub logs and stores visitors’ IP addresses for security purposes. See GitHub Pages documentation and the GitHub Privacy Statement for information about hosting data.

Privacy questions

Contact Houghton Audio at live-meters-support@houghtonaudio.com with questions about this policy.
